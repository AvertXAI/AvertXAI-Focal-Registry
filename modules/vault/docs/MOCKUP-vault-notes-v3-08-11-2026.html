Import — three surfaces, one reviewer
📄
Notes · Runbooks · Snippets

Pick one folder or many. Walks them, finds .md · .txt · .pdf only, reads YAML frontmatter with gray-matter, previews before writing.

📦
Repos

Point at a folder of repos. Each child folder with a .git becomes a repo row; its README.md is pulled in as the offline snapshot.

🌐
Infrastructure

Zone files, CSV exports, or a folder of them. Exact parse — no model, no guessing. Screenshot reading stays the fallback.

Why only .md, .txt and .pdf

Your rule, and it is the right one. A folder walk that accepts everything drags in node_modules, images and binaries, and the review list becomes unusable. The three types are what a runbook or note is actually written in. Everything else is listed as skipped, never silently ignored — you can see what was passed over.
