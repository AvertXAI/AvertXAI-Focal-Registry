The one thing I would push back on. Importing an .env copies live production keys into the vault, so they then exist in two places instead of one — and the vault becomes worth more to an attacker than the repo was. That is a fair trade if it saves you hunting for keys on a VPS at 2am, but it should be a choice you made on purpose rather than a side effect of adding a repo. Hence the three options on the first screen, and hence Skip this file being one of them.
